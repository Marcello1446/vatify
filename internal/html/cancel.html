How to cancel your subscription

You can cancel anytime from the billing portal — no emails, no calls. Your plan stays active until the end of your current billing period, then it simply won't renew.

1

Open your billing portal

Go to the stripe.com and login
Click Billing > Subscriptions below. You'll be taken straight to your secure billing portal — no separate login needed.

stripe
2

Find your active plan

In the portal, you'll see your current plan and next billing date at the top of the page. Click 3 dots nearby.

billing.stripe.com
3

Cancel your plan

Click Cancel subscription, then confirm on the dialog that follows. Stripe will ask if you're sure — this is your last chance to back out.

billing.stripe.com
4

You're done

Your API key keeps working at full plan limits until your current period ends, then it automatically drops to the Free tier — no need to do anything else.

Nothing breaks. Your existing API key stays valid. Once your plan ends, it simply moves to Free-tier limits (500 requests/month) instead of being disabled.

Common questions

Will I lose access immediately?

Depends on what you chose. Cancelling stops future billing, but you keep your current plan's limits until the period you already paid for ends or you stop your plan immediately based on your choice after you clicked 'Cancel subscription'.

What happens to my API key?

It keeps working. After your billing period ends, it automatically switches to Free-tier limits — you don't need to regenerate anything.

Can I resubscribe later?

Yes, anytime. Choose a plan again from the pricing page and your existing API key will be upgraded — no new key needed.

Do I get a refund for the unused part of my period?

Cancelling doesn't issue an automatic refund — you keep access for the period you already paid for instead. If you have a billing question outside that, contact support.

I can't find the appropriate link or button. What do I do?

Contact support and we'll help directly.
